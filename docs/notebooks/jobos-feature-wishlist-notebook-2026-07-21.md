# JobOS Feature Wishlist

A lightweight running list of potential JobOS features. Ideas belong here before planning or implementation.

## Job workspace

Give each job one shared workspace for its listing, research, notes, documents, and agent activity.

## Application checklist

Track the required steps and materials for each application through submission and follow-up.

## Interview mode

Create a focused workspace for company research, interview questions, preparation notes, and follow-ups.

## Freeform panel layout

Drag the Jobs, Browser/Document, and Agent sections into any order. Preserve the custom order, size, and collapsed state after reopening JobOS.

## Agent inbox

Show a short feed of jobs found, work completed, and decisions waiting for review.

## Compare jobs

Place two opportunities side by side to compare fit, compensation, risks, and priorities.

## Command palette and keyboard shortcuts

Quickly jump to jobs, tabs, layouts, documents, and common actions without reaching for the mouse.
